About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple tool for capturing and organizing ideas, reminders, and important notes. It helps you quickly record thoughts before you forget them.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea or reminder into the note field.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button to save your note.
  3. Review your notes in the notes list.

Features

  • Quickly capture ideas and reminders.
  • Simple and easy-to-use interface.
  • Organized list of your notes.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for faster note-taking.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + Enter Add a new note
Esc Clear the note input
